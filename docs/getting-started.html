Getting started with Stationwagon

Stationwagon is radio software for small stations. It runs on ordinary shared cPanel hosting: an AutoDJ with real crossfades, a stream for listeners, and a studio for live shows in the browser, on Windows or on Android. This guide takes you from an empty hosting account to your first live show.

Part 1 · for the admin

Set up the station

Once per station. About 20 minutes.

What Stationwagon does

Everything runs on your web host. There's no streaming server to rent, no extra ports and no program running all day.

  • The AutoDJ plans which song plays when, hours ahead, from your playlists and weekly schedule. Songs crossfade like on a real station.
  • Listeners open your station page (the player) or the small player you put on your own website. Everyone hears the same moment, 20 seconds behind the station's clock. That delay is what keeps the sound smooth on any connection.
  • DJs go live from the studio. Their show replaces the AutoDJ in their own time slot, and the AutoDJ takes over again when they end.
  • Every live show becomes a podcast episode that the DJ can trim and publish.

Make the database in cPanel Admin

  1. In cPanel, open MySQL Databases.
  2. Create a database, for example wordwave. cPanel puts your account name in front: controllco_wordwave.
  3. Create a database user with a strong password. Keep the password: the installer asks for it once.
  4. Under Add user to database, add the user to the database with All Privileges.
Already have a database?You can use an existing, even busy, database. Stationwagon gives its tables a prefix so they never mix with others, and the installer refuses a prefix that's already in use.

Put the files on the server Admin

Pick one way.

With SSH (from a PC with the repository)

bash tools/deploy-app.sh <ssh host> public_html/<your folder>

This copies the app, and leaves a one-time setup key in install.key on the server so nobody else can run your installer first. Run the same command for every later update: it replaces the code and never touches your settings or your songs.

With cPanel's File Manager

  1. Zip the contents of the app/ folder on your computer.
  2. In File Manager, open the folder for your station's address, upload the zip and Extract it there.
  3. Open the station's address straight away and install (next step). Without an install.key the installer doesn't ask for a key, so don't leave it waiting.

Run the installer Admin

Open your station's address in a browser. You land on Set up your station, which first shows the Server check. Fix any red rows; the installer can't continue until they pass.

  1. Setup key. If asked, copy it from install.key (next to index.php, in File Manager). It's deleted once setup is done.
  2. 1 · Database. The database name, user and password from cPanel. The host is usually localhost.
  3. 2 · Station. Your station's name and its timezone. The schedule and the station clock use it.
  4. 3 · Your sign-in. Your name, email and password. You become the station's first admin; you add managers and DJs later.

When it's done you see Go to the station.

One more step?If the host doesn't let the installer save its settings file, it shows the file's content instead. Create config.local.php next to index.php (File Manager → + File) with exactly that content, then press I've saved it, open the station. That file holds your database password, so don't share it.

Keep the AutoDJ planned Admin

Add one cron job so the timeline stays planned hours ahead, even when nobody is listening. In cPanel open Cron Jobs, choose Every 5 minutes and use this command, with your own account and folder:

php /home/<account>/public_html/<your folder>/cli/plan.php > /dev/null 2>&1

The same job fetches the free location database once a month, for the Listeners screen. To fill it the first time, run php cli/geo-update.php once over SSH, or wait for the job to do it.

Why a cron job?Listeners also keep the timeline planned when it runs short. The cron job makes sure it's ready before the first listener of the day arrives.

Updating the station Admin

Copy the new files over the old ones (the same deploy command, or File Manager). Your settings, songs and recordings stay. The database upgrades itself on the first page view after an update, so there's never a manual migration.

The Windows and Android studio apps update themselves when they open (never during a show).

Part 2 · for managers

Run the station

The manager is at /manage. Its menu on the left takes you to each screen.

Sign in and stay signed in Everyone

Go to your station's address and choose Sign in (or open /signin).

  • Keep me signed in on this device for 30 days: tick it on your own computer or phone, and you won't need your password there for 30 days. Leave it off on a shared computer.
  • Save your password: your browser, the Windows app (Save your password?) and the Android app (Save password to sign in to Stationwagon Studio?) offer to keep it in their password manager and fill it in next time.
  • Sign out (top right) ends the sign-in on that device only.
  • Change your password by selecting your name (top right). This signs you out on every other device.
Forgot your password?Ask the station's admin. They set a new one on the DJs screen.

Add DJs and managers Manager

  1. Open DJs.
  2. Add the person's name, email, role and a first password, and give them the password in person.
  3. They can change it on their account page after signing in.

On the same screen you change someone's role, set a new password for someone who forgot theirs, or remove them (tick Yes, remove to confirm). Removing someone also signs them out everywhere.

Add music Manager

Open Music library and drag MP3s onto the drop zone (or choose them). Each song is prepared in your browser before it uploads, so the server does no heavy work:

  • silence is trimmed at both ends, with short 20 ms fades;
  • Apply pre-processing (on by default) masters the song: a broadcast multiband compressor, then mastering, matched to the station's Adobe Audition chain. Mastered songs get a Mastered tag;
  • Loudness normalization (on by default) levels every song to −16 LUFS, so nothing jumps out louder or quieter;
  • it uploads a new MP3 (192 kbps unless you change it in Sound & safety) with the song's own tags.

Both switches sit above the drop zone and are saved for the station as soon as you change them. Keep the page open until the uploads finish: a minute of music takes about 2 seconds to master. Search, edit titles or remove songs in the list below.

No schedule yet?The AutoDJ plays the whole library until you add playlists and a schedule, so a station with songs is on air straight away.

Playlists Manager

  1. Open Playlists and make one, for example "Morning worship".
  2. Choose shuffle or in order.
  3. Add songs from the library and drag them into order.

A playlist can be switched off without deleting it. A song can be in many playlists.

The weekly schedule Manager

Open Schedule. It's a week grid in the station's timezone. Add two kinds of block:

  • Playlist blocks: which playlist the AutoDJ plays on which days and hours.
  • Live blocks: a show and its DJ. That DJ may go live from 5 minutes before the block starts until it ends.

Changes re-plan what isn't on air yet. A live block with nobody live is filled by the AutoDJ.

The Overview and Skip Manager

Overview is the manager's home: what's on air now and next, how many are listening, and a health list (timeline planned, music library, playlists and schedule). A green dot means fine.

Skip this song fades the current song out and starts the next. It does nothing while a live show is on air or about to start.

Listeners and privacy Manager

Listeners shows how many listen now, the most at once today, unique listeners per day and month, hours listened, and where people listen from by country, city, device, browser and app. Every chart has a table view.

Each Listen is kept for 30 days. The station's Privacy page (linked under the player) says what's kept and why. Set its contact address on the Listeners screen.

Not countedApps that play the stream's address directly (not your player or the embed) aren't counted.

Put the player on your website Manager

On the station page, open Put the player on your website, choose the dark or light look and press Copy. Paste that code into any web page: it shows a small player that plays the station 20 seconds behind, like the station page.

Other apps and websites can read what's playing (now, the next 3 songs, the last 5) from api/nowplaying.php.

Your podcast Manager

Open Podcast and fill in its details: title, description, cover picture, language and category. Published episodes appear on the station's podcast page (/podcast) and in its RSS feed (/podcast/feed), which you can submit to podcast apps.

Change the look Everyone

Every screen has a Look menu in its top bar: Green (the default) or Hi-fi (amber on graphite). It's your own choice, kept on that device, and it changes every screen at once.

Before the show DJ

  1. Wear headphones. Speakers feed back into the mic.
  2. Open the studio and press Switch on the studio. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
  3. Check your mic meter moves when you speak, and pick a preset in the microphone strip.
  4. Pick your Headphones output in the status strip, if your computer has more than one.
  5. Build your running order: search the library, add songs, station IDs and talk items. It's saved for your block, so you can prepare it days before.
  6. Load the first song to Deck B. Deck A will pick up the AutoDJ song when you go live.
When can I go live?From 5 minutes before your live block starts, until it ends. Outside your block the banner says why not. Managers and admins can go live any time.

Go live and end the show DJ

  1. Press Go live. Deck A picks up the AutoDJ's song exactly where it is, so listeners hear no jump.
  2. Talk with Mic on air, crossfade between the decks, and follow your running order. Opening the mic marks your next talk item as now (or press Now on it).
  3. When you're done, press Go live again and choose End show. The AutoDJ carries on.

Listeners hear you 20 seconds later. That's normal: don't wait for yourself in the station's player.

Notes for listeners DJ

Open Notes in the top bar to post Bible verses, references, video links or short notes. Listeners see each one beside the player at the moment of the show when you posted it, marked New.

  • A verse keeps its version (KJV unless you choose another) and links to Bible Gateway. Paste the verse text yourself.
  • Edit a posted note to fix it. It's posted again as new, and listeners see the change when they reach that moment.

The Windows app DJ

  1. In the browser studio on Windows, choose Get the Windows app and run the installer. No admin rights are needed.
  2. The first time, Windows SmartScreen may say "unknown publisher": choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Type your station's address and open the studio. Sign in; the app offers to save your password.

The Android app DJ

For Android 8 and newer.

  1. Sign in to the studio in your phone's browser and choose Get the Android app. Allow your browser to install it when Android asks.
  2. Open Stationwagon Studio. On its start page, type your station's address and press Save and open the studio.
  3. Sign in. Android offers to save your password; next time it offers Use your saved password.
  4. When you switch on the studio, allow Do Not Disturb, so calls and messages can't sound on air. The app turns it on (alarms only) when you go live and puts it back when you end.
  5. On the start page (App settings), allow Display over other apps so the app opens again by itself after an update. On a Xiaomi phone, also allow Autostart. In each list Android shows, find Stationwagon Studio and switch it on.
  • Your show keeps going with the screen off. A "You're on air" notification shows while you're live.
  • Back while on air asks before closing. App settings can't open until the show ends.
  • If the station can't be reached, the app shows its own page with Try again and Change the station.
Phones and tabletsWear headphones. A notification sound can reach the mic if Do Not Disturb is off.

After the show: your podcast episode DJ

Every live show over 2 minutes becomes a draft episode. The studio records your show on your device (the music and your mic as separate tracks) and uploads it after End show, so keep the studio open until the banner says the recording is uploaded.

  1. Open Podcast in the manager and choose your episode.
  2. The editor shows both tracks, split at every silence of 2 seconds or more. Click a clip and press Delete to cut it; Ctrl+Z undoes.
  3. Press Mixdown. It mixes what you kept and shortens long gaps to 1 second.
  4. Press Publish. The episode appears on the podcast page and in the feed.

The raw tracks are deleted 30 days after publishing. A show with no recording from your device (BUTT, or a reload) becomes an episode from what went on air.

Part 4

Help

Common questions

Listeners hear me 20 seconds late. Is something wrong?

No. Every listener is held 20 seconds behind the station clock, so the sound stays smooth and everyone hears the same moment.

I can't press Go live.

You can go live from 5 minutes before your live block. Check the time in the top bar and ask a manager to check the schedule.

The AutoDJ came back in the middle of my show.

Your sound stopped reaching the station for 8 seconds. Open the studio and press Rejoin your show.

The station page says nothing is planned.

Check that the library has songs and the cron job runs every 5 minutes.

A song starts too loud or too quiet.

Upload it again with Loudness normalization on in the Music library.

The Windows app says "unknown publisher".

That's expected the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

The Android app didn't reopen after an update.

Allow Display over other apps (and Autostart on Xiaomi) on the app's start page, then tap its icon this once.

I forgot my password.

Ask the station's admin to set a new one on the DJs screen.
